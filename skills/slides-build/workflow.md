# Steps 6–8 for decks: the three representative pages, the remaining pages, building the whole

Steps 6–8 of "Overall flow" in `../deck-practices/SKILL.md`, for decks only. Before this, run steps 1–5 (`../deck-practices/start.md`); after this, run step 9 (`/deck-review`) and steps 10–11 (`../deck-practices/deliver.md`). How each check layer works is slides-flow-005 in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).

## 6. Three representative pages (decks over 10 pages)

[slides-flow-007] **Three representative pages:** for decks over 10 pages, after the plan review, build only three body pages of different types (a diagram, a table or code, the page with the most text or elements, etc.) (write the page JSON, list only those three in `pages` of `deck.json`, and run `slides.py new`), and check them yourself before building the whole. Do not include the cover or section dividers. They are fixed types, so looking at them decides nothing. Do not ask for approval and do not wait. Share the URL as soon as it is created (deck-drive-003 in `../deck-practices/drive.md`; whether to look is up to the user). How to check:
   1. Bring the lint `要修正` of `gslides.py check <URL>` to 0 and open the three images yourself.
   2. [slides-flow-008] Ask a review child agent (`../deck-practices/agents.md`) to review the three pages. Write the request in the page-reviewer form of `../deck-review/prompt_slides.md` (aspects 2 and 5), and add at the top of "What to check": "List first the defects in the frame and in components that would repeat on every page using them (diagrams, tables, amount and size of text, margins; slides-visual-049)." For a rebuild or a port, also pass the images of the same pages in the original version and have it compare them (deck-review-003 in `../deck-review/ask.md`). While waiting, write the remaining pages in JSON (7).
   3. Classify the findings with "What to fix" in `../deck-review/fix.md`; fix those to fix and send those to ask to 10. Divide the fixes into three by how far they spread. Do not rebuild the three pages and do not rerun 1 and 2; go on to 7.
      - Fixes by type (component choice, arrangement, font size, and color that are the same on pages of the same type; table row height; tool constraints): write them one per line in the 「書き方の決め」 section of the outline, in one of two groups: 「デッキで共通」 for the frame that every page shares (positions of the heading, lead, and source line, text sizes, colors, how a component looks, names; slides-visual-049 in `visual.md`), and 「形ごと」 for lines that hold only on pages of one form, each starting with the form's name (「表:」「流れ図:」). Do not write which form a page uses there; that is the outline's type column, because a line like 「本文は 3 枚のカード」 spreads one form to every page. Apply them to the three pages' JSON before writing the remaining pages (before asking child agents) (they spread to every page, so always fix them before building the whole. Use `grep` to find the same component). For a fix that changes how something is drawn and that you are not sure works, run `slides.py lint` on that page; only when lint cannot tell, build the three pages and look at the images.
      - Fixes in writing (line break positions, characters per line in cards, the scope and number of headings, limiting words such as 「だけ」「すべて」, the first use of rephrased terms and abbreviations): also write them one per line in 「書き方の決め」 and apply them to the three pages before writing the rest.
      - Fixes for that page only: fix that page while writing the remaining pages in 7 (while waiting, if you asked child agents). Do not make type-level fixes while waiting (child agents copy the frame from the three pages).
      - The fixes to the three pages are built together with the whole in 8, and checked by `gslides.py check --all` on the whole and by `/deck-review`.
   4. [slides-flow-009] Add one section, in the form at its top, to the log (the workspace's `Research/deck_research/log/20261009_rep3_selection.md`. It is a per-material log, so write it in the workspace, and route back only what turns out to affect rules, following "Rule layers" in [rules.md](../deck-practices/rules.md). Keep this up for the next 10 materials, to see whether the application method in 8 and the card-height and last-line warnings added on 2026-10-13 worked) with: the three pages chosen and why, the findings and the fixes, and the number of 「書き方の決め」 lines and the number of pages they were applied to. Also write it in the report in 10, in up to three lines. This log is used to review the selection guide and the application method in 8.
- After writing the log, call the context-summarizing tool if you can (deck-flow-005).

Evidence: previously we passed the URL and waited for approval of the three pages, but they were approved without being looked at (in 12 past approvals, 0 replies touched the content of the three pages; `Research/deck_rep3_approval_sessions_20261009.md`). We compare with the original version in a rebuild because rounded corners and icons once decreased from the original and both the human approval and the review missed it. The three pages are not rebuilt because, in 48 logged materials, a second check newly found defects spreading to every page in only 2 materials, 3 cases (all side effects of line-break fixes), and the second check took 10–19 minutes per material (`20261009_rep3_selection.md`, counted on 2026-10-17). The context after the three pages is 230k–340k tokens at the median, and the building steps alone add about 500k (2026-10-10; `Research/pi_compaction_deck_workflow_20261010/README.md`). AI 実践 1-3.

## 7. Write the remaining pages

For decks of 10 pages or fewer, write the whole deck here. For decks that went through the three representative pages, write the remaining pages in `build/p<number>.json`, copying only the frame from the three pages' JSON and choosing each page's form from what it says (slides-visual-048, 049; slides-pages-034 in `pages.md`). Keep only the three pages listed in `pages` of `deck.json`; just write the rest to files (they are listed when building the whole in 8). Run `slides.py lint` on the pages you wrote and bring `要修正` to 0. For each `出典の表にない数字`, add the row to the 「出典の表」 with its source first, or use the table's value (deck-content-064).

- [slides-flow-016] **Split the remaining pages among child agents (decks only).** You may have child agents write the remaining pages in parallel, with the parent listing and building them. The parent decides whether and how to split. Do not split decks of 30 pages or fewer, or when the remainder is not enough for two child agents (the child agents' startup time cancels the gain). Split by chapter (section divider) or by groups of a few pages in the outline, and give pages of the same type or the same diagram to the same child agent. The biggest risk is variation in writing between child agents; hold it down with item 1 below and the checks in 8. Documents are not split, because the parent writes the single doc.md.
  1. Before asking, write out all of the 「書き方の決め」 fixed in step 3 of 6 in the outline, apply them to the three pages' JSON, and add the numbers and phrasings that must be the same across pages (names, the page where an abbreviation first appears). For things that appear on pages of two or more assignees (the same tool, mechanism, or tier), decide down to the one-line explanation and write it. Child agents copy only the frame from the three representative pages' JSON (slides-visual-049) and do not rewrite them. In a split deck, what the parent writes while waiting for the three-page review is the draft of 「書き方の決め」 and the request texts; the child agents write the remaining pages.
  2. Ask one child agent per assignment with `../slides-build/prompt_pages.md`. The child agent writes its pages in JSON, one file per page (`build/p<number>.json`), runs `slides.py lint` on a list `build/pages_<owner>.json` without building, and returns. Have it write anything that changes claims, numbers, or order, and anything the rules do not decide, as the outline says, and return it to the parent.
  3. When they return, list the child agents' pages in `pages` of `deck.json` in outline order (slides-pages-031 in `pages.md`) and go on to 8. Of what the child agents returned, send the items to ask to 10, and write the forms they changed into the outline's type column. In the three-representative-pages log, write how you split (number of child agents and their pages).

Evidence: the step where the parent writes alone in order ("write the whole and build") takes a median of 17 minutes per material (14 materials); splitting among child agents is expected to save about 10 minutes on decks over 30 pages (estimate; 「候補と見込み」 ⑤ in the outline in the workspace's `Internal/deck_speed_202610/`). In a 13-page trial, all 4 cases of variation across writers were in things that appeared on pages of two or more assignees.

## 8. Build and check the whole deck

[slides-flow-011] **Before building the whole, apply what was fixed in 6 to the remaining pages.** After writing the remaining pages in JSON, go through 「書き方の決め」 line by line and fix them against the headings, cards, and table cells of every page (for headings, recount the tables and cards in the body; for rephrased terms and abbreviations, search `build/*.json`). Also apply the component fixes made on the three pages (e.g. omitting a card's `h` and sizing it from its content) to the same components on the remaining pages. If you build without applying them, the defects found on the three pages appear unchanged on the remaining pages.

After applying them, list all pages in `pages` of `deck.json` and build once to the same URL (`slides.py build`; for decks that skipped 6, create the deck here with `slides.py new`). Then run the whole-deck layer of checks (slides-flow-005 in `SKILL.md`).

- Look at each build warning (`slides.py` prints them to stderr: a last line of 2 characters or fewer, content that does not fit in `h`, etc.) one by one.
- Bring the `要修正` of `gslides.py check <URL> --all` to 0 and look at each `要確認` one by one.
- For decks written by split child agents, find and fix variation between child agents (size and position of same-type components, characters in cards, phrasing) with the warnings, a search of `build/*.json`, and the contact sheet.
- In the contact sheet of all pages (`sheet.py`), check the size and position of same-type components, the density, and the color.
- Do not open each page at full size. The full-size images are viewed by the review child agent in round 1 of 9. The parent opens at full size only the pages flagged above (the case in slides-flow-005 for building the whole for the first time and going straight into `/deck-review`).

Evidence: in 21 logged materials, about 60% of the every-page defects found after building the whole, within the range visible on the three pages, were cases where the fixes on the three pages had not been applied to the remaining pages (the application method in 8 and the card-height and last-line warnings were added on 2026-10-13).
