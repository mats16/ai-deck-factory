# What gslides.py check and lint.py do

Open when you read the output of `gslides.py check` or `slides.py lint`, or when deciding whether a `要確認` may stay.

## What gslides.py check does

1. Writes the `presentations.get` JSON to `/tmp/slides_<first 8 chars of the ID>/deck.json`
2. Writes the text and notes of each page to `deck.md` (the text you pass to reviews)
3. Runs `lint.py` (below) on the JSON
4. Takes images (Google-rendered thumbnails, 1600 × 900) of pages changed since the last run into `p-NN.png` (6 in parallel. Waits before 60 per minute). Pages are identified by objectId (`slides.py build` creates only pages whose content changed, with new IDs, so the same ID means the same content). Other pages keep their previous images; images whose page number shifted are moved (only the page number at the bottom right is old). If someone edited in the UI after the last build, all pages. `--all` for all pages, `--pages 3,5` for only those pages
5. `box_pad.py` measures, on the images it took (laying out each page), the gap between top-aligned text of 2 or more lines inside a filled box and the bottom edge of the box, and reports gaps narrower than one character as 「要確認（画像で測った）」 (visual.md §4. Line height cannot be estimated from the JSON, so it is measured on images. 2026-10-09). For text right above the bottom edge of a white card, it checks for one character even for a 1-line tag (such as 「2 回まで」) (2026-10-14). It also reports top-aligned text in a single standalone light panel where the space below is 0.25 in or more larger than the space above (center it or shrink the panel. 2026-10-10)

6. Writes the page numbers it took, the image paths, and that page's lint and box_pad findings to `changed.json` (`slides.py build` also writes the pages it rebuilt to the same file). Prints the seconds per step and the 429 waits on one line to standard error (build does too)

If the lint has `要修正`, it returns exit 1 after taking the images.

## What lint.py does

Written rules are easy to break, so whatever a machine can check is counted here (standard library only. It also runs alone as `python3 lint.py <deck.json>`). The content and sources of the rules are in `deck-practices`. Theme colors are resolved to actual colors with the deck master's color scheme.

- **List of headings:** Prints the largest text on each page in order, with the lead indented below it if there is one. If it likely does not fit on one line, it adds 「2 行の見込み」. Read it through and check that the story holds (content.md in `deck-practices`).
- **Body character count:** Prints, per page, the number of characters excluding the heading, lead, source line, and footer. With `--talk` (talks such as LTs), it reports pages over 125 characters as `要確認` (content.md §2).
- **`要修正` (exit 1):** emoji, long dashes (—), leftover placeholders (TODO, 〇〇, lorem, and so on), the tag on placeholder pages for replacing internal material (「差し替え：」), slide text under 11pt (text inside a filled shape: under 9pt [slides-visual-037. Text layered on another shape's fill, such as card body text: under 11pt]; the confidentiality label on the same line as the copyright footer: also under 9pt. Decks whose name contains 「差し替え用」 keep the original page size, so they are not checked), fonts other than Noto Sans JP (Cascadia Mono with only alphanumerics passes), Japanese text in Cascadia Mono (monospace), Japanese periods (「。」) in headings (including mid-sentence ones), and headings that end in 「〜ください」「〜します」「〜しましょう」 (say requests in the lead or body. content.md §6).
- **`要確認`:** empty phrases (such as 「〜を実現する」), fear-mongering phrasing, uppercase English labels (monospace code lines are not counted), missing spaces between Japanese and Latin text, spaces around punctuation marks, leftover 「要確認」 or 「TBD」, confidentiality labels (Internal Only, Confidential, and so on. Those in the footer are combined into 1 finding), 「です・ます」 in slide body text, decks with more than 3 assertive headings (counts headings that contain a Japanese comma (「、」) and end in kana that look like a verb or adjective. Section dividers and the cover are not counted. content.md §4), mixed use of periods across bullets in one frame, numbers on pages without a source line, numbers whose digits are not in the 「出典の表」 (`出典の表にない数字`, deck-lint-026: `slides.py lint` reads the table from the material's folder, the parent of `build/`; off when that folder has neither an outline nor `references.md`), source lines without links, body text over 8 lines (neither counts monospace code boxes. `source:` is not treated as a source line), a heading or lead likely on 2 lines, automatic line breaks likely inside a phrase (it computes the break positions with the same character-width estimate as the build and reports those not at a phrase boundary from BudouX [called separately with `uv run --with budoux`]. It does not count breaks before punctuation or closing brackets that cannot start a line, breaks between alphanumeric words, table cells, footers, paragraphs that are only monospace, or text of unknown size [inherited from placeholders]. Being an estimate, it can be off by one character, so check on the images. Not counted if uv or BudouX is unavailable. slides-visual-013), horizontal arrows not at the vertical center of the boxes on both sides (if there are 2 or more between the same 2 boxes, the offset is intended and is not counted), overflow below the frame, text that reaches the text in the frame below (when a heading frame with a fixed height wraps. slides-lint-025), grids made of lines (3 or more vertical lines with the same top and length. Thin rectangles count as lines. Arrows and lines inside a group of rounded enclosures are not counted. Use a table), empty space below the body (1.0 in or more down to the conclusion band or 6.60 in), boxes larger than their content (0.6 in or more of a filled box below its text. Shapes without text, such as the components that make rounded corners or frame lines, do not count as content), holes in the middle of the body (0.65 in or more between elements or above the conclusion band, 1.0 in or more above the body. Below tables is not counted), large paragraphs of only a number (28pt or more with no unit), misaligned top or bottom edges of white cards in adjacent columns (0.03–1.0 in), Google's default dotted rounded rectangle with round corners (its curvature does not match the cards inside. In JSON, `box` with `"shape": "round_rectangle"` and `"dash": true`), conclusion bands taller than one third of the body, decks whose first page is not a cover (navy background), decks of more than 12 pages without section dividers (navy background), and chapters with only 1 page (or none) after the section divider.
- **Estimated and confirmed:** Findings derived from estimates of character width and line height (a heading or lead on 2 lines, body text over 8 lines, a line break inside a phrase, overlapping text that overflows its frame, empty space below the body, inside a box, or in the middle of the body) are marked 「（見積もり）」 (`ESTIMATE` in `lint.py`). Confirm them on the Google-rendered images. Everything else is fixed by the JSON.
- **Build warnings** (printed by slides.py. The list and how to fix them are in `commands.md`): check them one by one, like `要確認`.
- **Distribution of font sizes**, and **color pairs below the contrast threshold** (4.5:1 for regular text under 18pt and bold under 11pt; 3:1 for 18pt and up and bold 11pt and up. The fill under the text is used as the background. Text on images is not checked).
- Document rules (Google Docs) are in `../docs-build/lint.md`. The text rules (from emoji to leftover 「要確認」) are shared with slides.
- The Japanese language tag (`ja-JP`) cannot be set with the Slides API, so it is not counted (api.md).
- **Log:** `gslides.py check` and `docs.py check` write the `要確認` to `.factory/lint_runs.jsonl`, one line per run (no text from the material, only hashes of the findings). `python3 lint.py --stats` prints, per rule, how many findings appeared and how many remained until the last check of that material (used in step 5 of `dreaming/run.md`).
