# Slides API pitfalls

Open when you change `slides.py`, write raw requests, or a build fails in an unexpected way.

## Slides API pitfalls

Checked on 2026-10-07.

- **`presentations.create` ignores `pageSize` and makes 10 × 5.625 in.** Always create by copying the template (`slides.py new`).
- **When the page count reaches 0, unused masters are deleted along with their layouts.** Delete previous pages only after creating new ones (`build` does this).
- **The Japanese language (`ja-JP`) cannot be set** (there is no field for it in text style). Leave line-breaking rules (kinsoku) to Google Slides' typesetting, and check on images for punctuation at line starts.
- [slides-api-014] **The inner padding of a text frame is 0.1 in and cannot be changed. The corner radius of Google's standard rounded rectangle also cannot be set directly.** Shared components compose rectangles and circles to make rounded rectangles with a fixed radius. Filled boxes with an outline (white cards etc.) do not draw a line; they make the outline by overlaying, on a rounded rectangle of the line color, a filled rounded rectangle shrunk inward by the line weight (0.75 pt). Joining circle outlines and side lines produced a 1–2 px step at the seams in Google's rendering. Unfilled rounded enclosures (`d.box(…, line=…, kind="ROUND_RECTANGLE")`) cannot fill the inside, so they are drawn with lines for the sides and Google arcs (`ARC`; by default a quarter circle at the top right) flipped and placed at the 4 corners. When the arcs were joined from 8 short lines, they looked angular and had steps at the seams. Even with arcs, Google draws lines and shape outlines differently, so depending on position a 1 px step remains at corners (it did not go away when replacing flips with rotations, or when making the sides shape outlines. 2026-10-08, measured on the template sample p.5). The default radius for `box`, filled `text`, and `card` is 0.1 in. If the short side is under 0.2 in, the radius is limited to half the short side. Explicit `kind="ROUND_RECTANGLE"` also gets this radius. Use `radius=None` only for things like small line drawings that need Google's standard shape. `kind="RECTANGLE"` stays a rectangle. Changing shared components does not change saved materials; it is applied the next time they are rebuilt with `slides.py build`. Other sessions reload the fixed tools at their next generation.
- [slides-api-015] **Add dotted lines with `dash=True`. Dotted rounded rectangles are still drawn with radius 0.1 in.** Unfilled rounded rectangles are made of 4 side lines and 4 corner arcs, so those 8 get the dash and weight. When there are both a fill and a dotted outline, a dotted outline is overlaid on the filled rounded rectangle (the method of bordering a filled rounded rectangle cannot be dotted). Adding a dash to Google's standard rounded rectangle (`radius=None`) makes the radius about 1/6 of the short side, and the curvature does not match the cards inside. Red emphasis enclosures use 2pt lines whether dotted or solid (they are lines for emphasis, so do not make them thin). `d.box` and `d.text` use 2pt when `weight` of a red line is omitted. Even at 2pt, a 1 px pale dot remains at the seam between line and arc (visible at 4× zoom; hardly noticeable at 1×). Samples are on the template's component pages (page 4 is a solid line on one table column, page 5 is dotted).
- **Bullet dots are drawn about half the text size to the left of the first line's indent** (6 pt at 11pt, 7.2 pt at 14pt; measured on 2026-10-08). With 0 indent, the dot sticks out beyond the text's left edge in the frame. `d.text(…, bullets=True)` indents the first line by half the text size so that the dot's left edge aligns with the body's left edge.
- Text positions are counted in UTF-16 units (emoji etc. count as 2). `u16` in `slides.py`.
- The ID of the speaker notes frame is known only after the page is created. `build` writes notes in a second batchUpdate.
- [slides-api-016] Text that overflows its frame is not clipped. lint only checks bodies over 8 lines, so check overflow on images. Whether manually broken lines fit the frame is warned at build time using the `em_width` estimate. The estimate is not accurate to one character, so check lines over 98% of the frame width on images.
- Adding a link makes the text the theme's link color (red) unless a color is written. `d.text` writes the surrounding color.
- Inner padding of table cells cannot be changed via the API.
- **The API accepts font names even for fonts that do not exist, and silently renders another font.** `Noto Sans CJK JP` (the docx font), `Hiragino Sans`, and `Yu Gothic` became Mincho and a Latin serif. Japanese fonts in Google Fonts (BIZ UDPGothic, M PLUS 2, IBM Plex Sans JP etc.) render (`Research/google_slides_jp_fonts_20261007.md`). lint stops with `要修正` on fonts other than the template's font (default Noto Sans JP) (the monospace font for code [default Cascadia Mono, the theme's `mono`] passes for alphanumerics only, and stops when Japanese is included).
- [slides-api-017] **If the Google Fonts version of a monospace font has ligatures, Google Slides renders them, and the API cannot turn them off.** JetBrains Mono's `->` became a single arrow character. Code uses Cascadia Mono, which has no ligatures (`MONO`; it can be changed with the theme's `mono`, but `LIGATURE_MONO` fonts with ligatures stop), applied with `mono=True` and `mono_cols`. `mono_reqs` also makes leading indentation spaces monospace (if the spaces stay in Noto Sans JP, a 4-character indent shrinks to about 1.5 characters). Character width is estimated with `MONO_EM` (0.586 em; the Cascadia Mono value, not changed by the theme's `mono`) (`Research/monospace_font_google_slides_20261009.md`).
- [slides-api-018] **Checks use thumbnails (`pages.getThumbnail`) only, not PDF export.** 12 pages take about 5 s (6 in parallel), as fast as exporting to PDF and converting to images. It is the same rendering as the Google Slides UI, can be re-fetched per page, has no 10 MB limit of Drive export, and needs no poppler. Thumbnails count against the expensive read quota (per-minute limit), so `gslides.py` waits and retries when 429 is returned. Thumbnail URLs expire in 30 minutes.
- Drive `files.export` stops at 10 MB (403 「This file is too large to be exported.」). When pptx is needed, use `gslides.py export` (exports from `exportLinks`; it also exported the 14.6 MB 65-page internal template).
- **Exported pptx has language `en` even for Japanese text** (`../deck-research/sources.md`). If asked to hand pptx to a customer, after exporting, align fonts and language with the workspace's `Archive/pptx_pipeline_20261007/skills/office-jp/scripts/fixjp.py --all` (without the language, PowerPoint puts punctuation at line starts). Ask the user before handing it over.
