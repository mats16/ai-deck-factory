# Images, logos, and icons

Open when a page needs an image, a logo, an icon, or a screenshot.

## Images, logos, and icons

- [slides-image-019] **For images and icons, just pass a URL or a local path to `d.image` or `d.icon`.** How they are inserted (the temporary pptx below) is handled inside `slides.py build`, so do not worry about it when writing pages. Use it as is even for requests that say 「pptx を介さず」 (the deck itself does not go through pptx). Do not omit meaningful icons. Simple line drawings may be built as editable shapes with `d.box` and `d.arrow(..., head=False)`, but do not substitute custom shapes for official product logos or complex images.
- [slides-image-020] **For where to get them, prefer what can be inserted by URL as is.** Sources and URL forms are in `assets/README.md` (the ledger of assets inside this skill; when you add one, also write it in the ledger. Images only you use go in the workspace's `assets/`; `asset()` looks there first). Search in this order.
  1. Pass public PNG, JPG, or GIF URLs directly to `d.image` (AWS and Azure architecture icons from mingrammer/diagrams, photos from Unsplash, etc.).
  2. Icons and product logos are `d.icon(s, "tabler:database", x, y, 0.4)` (Iconify).
  3. [slides-image-021] Things distributed only as files (current Google Cloud icons, your own company's product icons) go in `assets/` directly under the workspace and are passed as `asset("gcp/bigquery.png")`. Do not copy them into the material folder (so they can be reused across materials).
  4. Images for that material only (screenshots, diagrams from a reused deck) go in `build/assets/`.
- [slides-image-022] **The Slides API can read only PNG, JPG, and GIF by URL.** Passing an SVG URL to `d.image` stops. Use PNG URLs first, and when SVG fits better (the PNG is too small to place large, no PNG is distributed, you want to change the color), switch to `png(<SVG URL>, w=1024)` (a URL where wsrv.nl renders it to PNG). Local SVGs (`.svg` in `assets/`) are converted to PNG at build time, so they can be passed as is.
- `d.image(s, <path or URL>, x, y, w, h)`. The image fits in the frame keeping its aspect ratio and is centered in the frame. To align it left or top, give the frame the same ratio as the image.
- [slides-image-023] **Local images (`assets/`, `build/assets/`) are inserted via Drive at build time (`carry` in `slides.py`).** The Slides API can read only images at public URLs (URLs of private Drive files are refused as not 「publicly accessible」). `build` makes a pptx with pptxgenjs holding one image per page, uploads it to the root of My Drive as Google Slides, inserts from that image's URL, and deletes the uploaded file (no folder is specified, so the script works as is when given to others). It does not change sharing settings to make anything public. Drive has no equivalent of S3 presigned URLs (sharing expirations can be set only for specific people and groups, and do not apply to APIs fetching anonymously). Google Cloud Storage signed URLs also work, but are not used (the speedup is only about 4 s for builds with images, which does not justify keeping a bucket and a signing SA, not working when given to others, and putting images in the sandbox. `Research/gcs_signed_url_images_20261008.md`). Drive's `thumbnailLink` is refused by the Slides API. The URL passed remains in the deck as the image's `sourceUrl`.
- To paste a page of another Google Slides as is, pass `thumb_url(<URL>, <page number>)` to `d.image` (`reuse.md` Stage 1).
- **Customer logos** are taken from case-study pages your company has published, or from that company's official press kit (it is easy to confirm the permitted scope of use). Do not download them; pass the URL through `png()` and insert with `d.image(s, png("<SVG URL>"), …, alt="<company name>")`. The Splunk logo is the PNG URL of `Splunk_logo.svg` on Wikimedia Commons (width is the fixed value in the ledger; images on splunk.com could not be fetched directly).
- [slides-image-024] **Icons and product logos** are placed by Iconify name, like `d.icon(s, "tabler:database", x, y, 0.4)` (find names at https://icon-sets.iconify.design/; if a name does not exist, it stops before building). `tabler:` is line art in one color, the body color `303F47` (default). One-color logos are `simple-icons:`, with the brand color passed as `color` (e.g. `d.icon(s, "simple-icons:postgresql", x, y, 0.4, "4169E1")`). Colored logos are `logos:` (for the mark only, `logos:<name>-icon`; `color` has no effect). The alt text gets the name. Pass `color` as 6-digit hex. `color` also accepts role names (`NAVY`, `SUB` etc.). Image colors cannot point to theme colors, so `d.icon` converts them to 6-digit hex from the deck's color scheme (the color scheme is taken from the theme file. For decks without a theme file, `build`, and `lint`, `dry`, and `resolve` given a URL, read it from the deck's master. Otherwise the visual color scheme). react-icons names from older pages.py (`TbDatabase`, `SiPostgresql`) also build as is (converted to PNG locally at build time).
- [slides-image-025] **When your company's product icon is not in the workspace's `assets/`, before substituting line art (`tabler:`), search the internal icon collection (where to search is in the workspace's `rules/slides-build/images.md`), and if found, add it to `assets/` and its ledger.** For images with a white rounded background and a shadow, remove the background and shadow to make them transparent. Use line art only for what is not found, and write it in the outline (checked in review aspect 5 of `../deck-review/prompt_slides.md`, "is a product replaced by a generic line icon").
- **Your company's product icons** go in the workspace's `assets/<company>/` and are placed with `d.image(s, asset("<company>/<product>.png"), …, alt="<product>")`. Other companies' products are `logos:` (Snowflake, Oracle, Salesforce, Tableau, Power BI, Redshift, etc.). Google Cloud products such as BigQuery are missing from `logos:` or have old artwork, so use `asset("gcp/<product>.png")`.
- For similar diagrams, borrow the ideas of past materials and rebuild them (search with `references_index.md`).
