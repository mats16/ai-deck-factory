# Requests after delivery

Open when a request comes in after a material was delivered (a correction, a question, comments, a change of audience), in the same or a new conversation. The rules for making in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) still apply.

[deck-flow-017] **For a request after delivery, first classify its type, then run only as far as that type's row says.** Requests after delivery are not only page fixes. There are also questions about the content (「この数字はどこから」「なぜこの順か」), research to add, and changes of audience. Running steps 8–11 for every one of them means building the whole and running `/deck-review` even for a wording fix. If asked in a new conversation, first read the history as in 1 and 2 (the outline, the internal memo, and the material's findings via `ledger.py list --deck <material folder>`). If one message mixes types, answer the questions first and run the fixes in the heavier row. Separate small fixes from content fixes by whether the page's point (what its heading claims), the numbers, or the number and order of pages change. A replacement where the user gave the exact wording follows the instruction even if the point changes, so treat it as a small fix if the page count and order do not change (numbers are a content/structure fix even when the user gives the value, so that you verify the source and apply it to the same number on other pages); if it is a heading, the parent re-reads the heading flow in `deck.md`. If unsure how to classify, choose the heavier one.

| Type | Examples | What to do | Build and check | Review child agent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Question | The source of this number, why this order, what this term refers to, whether there are other options | Verify with the outline's source table, `references.md`, and the sources, and answer. Do not change the material. If the answer reveals an error in the material (a number, a source, an overstatement), attach a proposed fix and ask whether to fix it (deck-flow-010) | No | Do not start one |
| Small fix | Wording, line breaks, color, size, arrangement that does not change a diagram's meaning, unifying notation, a replacement with the wording specified | Fix that page's JSON (for documents, doc.md) | lint → build once → the parent looks at the images of the changed pages (`changed.json`). If there are many, look at the contact sheet and open only the flagged pages at full size | Do not start one |

If sources are needed, gather them as in 3 and 4 with `refs.py add`, and add the facts to the 「出典の表」 before the pages. | Major fix | Rearrange the material's storyline (the opening summary and the order of headings) (「結論から先に」「事例から入る」), or change more than half of the pages | If something missing from the request (which case study, what to recommend) changes the storyline, ask first. If sources are needed, gather them as in 3 and 4. Rewrite the outline as `構成案_v2.md`, run the plan review as `plan_reviewer` says (deck-flow-004 in 5; pass the original request plus this message), then run 6–9 in the same order as when making the material, and clean up in 11 (the three representative pages in 6 may be skipped if the page types do not change). Deliver in the form of 10 plus what was checked as in "Deliver" below. - **Telling questions from fixes.** Statements of a problem (「分かりにくい」「〜に寄り過ぎている」「〜は要らない」「逆向きでは」) are treated as fix requests. If you verify and the current version is correct, do not fix it; answer with the reason. Statements asking for an opinion (「この順でいい？」) get an answer and a proposed fix, and you ask whether to fix. Items where you only answered a question are not written to the rule ledger. Items written to the ledger as fix requests but not fixed after verifying get `ledger.py route <c-ID> 戻さない`.
- [deck-flow-018] **In a conversation making or editing a material, when told 「図解して」「図にして」, add a page to that material and draw it** (a content/structure fix). Do not settle for HTML in the chat or a diagram in the reply. Ask only when what to diagram is not decided (once a chat diagram was returned, and the user restated that they meant adding it to the slides).
- **Apply changed terms and numbers to other pages too.** For every type of fix, search `build/*.json` (for documents, `build/doc.md`) for the changed terms and numbers, fix them also on pages and summaries that were not mentioned, and list the extra pages on delivery.
- **The rules for making still apply after delivery.** Do not silently change confirmed claims, numbers, or order; when something does not fit, consider rephrasing, diagramming, and splitting in that order, and if you split, ask on delivery (deck-flow-010).
- **Build once.** Put in all the fixes and bring the `要修正` of `slides.py lint` to 0 before building (the checkpoint in slides-flow-005). Only if the images show a fix is needed, fix it and build once more.
- **For documents,** read "changed pages" in the table as "changed sections". After building, check with `docs.py check`; page breaks move, so view the images of all pages in the contact sheet and open the pages of the changed sections at full size (§3 of `../docs-build/SKILL.md`; there is no `changed.json` or `check --all`). Give the review child agent the full text of `text.md` and the headings of the changed sections.
- **When asking a review child agent,** use the page-reviewer form of `../deck-review/prompt_slides.md` and pass the `changed.json` entries of the changed pages (including added pages), the full `deck.md`, and the outline. If the page count or order changes, `changed.json` covers all pages, so note the changed pages' numbers as numbered after building and extract only those entries. What the page reviewer returns as 「全体へ」 goes to the whole-deck child agent if you started one; if not, the parent checks it in `deck.md` (for documents, use `prompt_doc.md`, as in the row above) (how to ask: `agents.md`, and "How to ask" in `../deck-review/SKILL.md`). Add 「直したこと」 (one line per page) to the request. Verify the findings by step 4 of deck-review, and fix them after classifying them with "What to fix". The child agents for a content/structure fix (the changed-page reviewer, and the whole-deck child agent if started) stop after one round; after fixing, the parent looks at the images and delivers (do not ask them again). `/deck-review` for major and premise fixes runs up to the round limit as in 9. Write the table to `$D/review.md` and import it with `ledger.py review` (step 7 of deck-review).
- **Except for questions, route back to the ledger and the rules** (「修正の指示」 in AGENTS.md; how to choose the destination: [rules.md](rules.md)). Put `ledger.py add` and the rule edits into one call (one `bash`). Write in the outline only when the policy changed (content/structure, major, or premise fixes), and do not copy the correction itself there (it is in the ledger; "Routing corrections" in rules.md).
- **If the speaker notes changed,** update the video made from the deck, if any, without asking; if the pages changed, say instead that the video no longer matches ("When to make or update a video" in `../vids-build/SKILL.md`).
- **Deliver.** The URL (rebuilding does not change it), the pages fixed and what was fixed, what was checked (what was run, and why if no child agent was started), the questions for the user, and which correction was routed to which rule. If you only answered a question, the answer and the source are enough. Before delivering, call the context-summarizing tool if you can (deck-flow-005).

Evidence: 67 revision requests after delivery (2026-10-09 to 10-19, pi logs; the workspace's `Internal/deck_revision_speed_20261019/`) took a median of 13.2 minutes and $2.41 each. The 26 that started a review child agent took 23.9 minutes and $3.76; the 41 that did not took 8.2 minutes and $1.90. Of 19 fixes that pointed at a page, 4 started a child agent, and in 2 of them the child agent raised 「直す」 (4 in total). Of the 35 child agents, including those for fixes that did not point at a page, 26 raised at least one 「直す」, so dropping child agents entirely is risky. The median number of builds per request was 3, and writing to the ledger, the rules, and the outline took about 3 minutes per request.
