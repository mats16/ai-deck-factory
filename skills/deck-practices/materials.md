# Gathering materials

Read this when gathering internal materials and customer information (`SKILL.md` "How to start" steps 3 and 4, and 「調べもの」 in AGENTS.md). Handle confidential information as 「社外秘と顧客の情報」 in AGENTS.md says. Record what you read in `references.md` in the material's folder (see "Ledger" below).

- [materials-001] **Do not stall waiting on slow tools.** Two rules for waiting:
  - [materials-002] **One command runs at most 5 minutes.** Set the limit with the tool's `timeout` (the argument of pi's bash or Claude Code's Bash). macOS has no `timeout` command. When a command hits the limit, do not rerun the same command. Narrow the scope (period, path, `git clone --depth 1`, only the needed files through the GitHub API) or postpone it.
  - [materials-003] **Report on a subagent at 20 minutes.** Do not wait with `sleep`. Move ahead with other materials and the skeleton of the outline, and add the result when it arrives (`SKILL.md` "Working practices", deck-practices-013). If there is no result 20 minutes after delegating, tell the user what you are waiting for, since when, and what you are doing meanwhile (do not wait for approval).

## Internal materials (reuse and messaging)

The tools are listed by role in the workspace's `factory.toml` under `[sources]` (`search`, `chat`, `meetings`, `wiki`, `crm`, `usage`). For a role that is not listed, ask the user to paste the material or URL. How to use each tool and where to look in that company are in the workspace's `rules/deck-practices/materials.md`.

- **Internal cross search** (`search`): search here first for materials on the same theme (「<製品名> pitch deck」「<テーマ> 日本語」「<顧客の業種> 事例」 and so on).
- **File storage**: read the contents of the Google Slides and Docs you found (for Google Slides, `../deck-build/scripts/gslides.py` also works).
- **Internal chat** (`chat`): product channels and field channels hold the latest wording, cautions, and constraints that the docs do not mention. When you find a constraint that is not in the docs, write it in customer-facing materials only as far as the docs support. Bug details, answers, and future plans are internal information, so write them in `社内メモ_*.md`.
- **Internal wiki** (`wiki`): enablement materials and guides for the field. If there is a list of Japanese materials, cite the official Japanese pages.
- **How to reuse:** try in this order: use as is → crop figures and similar parts → rebuild in this folder's style while keeping the layout → take only the intent and make it new. How to decide and how to do it are in [reuse.md](reuse.md).

## Customer information

- **Meeting records** (`meetings`): read both meetings with the customer and internal meetings with sales. Read the summary first, and look at the transcript only when you need evidence.
- **Internal chat** (`chat`): search by customer name, and look at the internal account channel and the channel shared with the customer. Day-to-day exchanges that meeting records miss are here.
  - [materials-004] When a colleague pastes comments from another company or the customer (a transcription such as 「いただいた問い合わせの内容もシェアします」), confirm whose words they are before interpreting them. Do not read a transcription as the colleague's own remark, and do not add your own reading such as 「聞き違い」. For words whose meaning you do not know (such as 「ブロック ID」), write that you do not know, and add them to what needs checking.
  - [materials-005] When a link the tools cannot read (such as a separate workspace shared with the customer) is given as background for the request, ask the user to paste its text in your first reply. Until it arrives, do not fix the audience and the questions in the outline. When it arrives, copy the projects, teams, and areas the customer wrote into the top of the outline (if you proceed without reading the text, information about the project under consideration is lost).
- **Customer ledger** (`crm`): deals, owners, and basic customer information.
- **Usage and consumption** (`usage`): note each number with its source (which table, which period, which unit). Usage amounts are internal information, so do not write them in customer-facing materials (「社外秘と顧客の情報」 in AGENTS.md).

## Ledger (references.md and the 「出典の表」)

Keep two tables joined by IDs: sources in `references.md` (S<number>) and the facts the pages state in the 「出典の表」 (F<number>). With them you find a source again without searching, write a page without guessing a value, and check a value without rereading the conversation. Write the rows with `scripts/refs.py` and keep the columns as they are, because lint and the source-check child read them by column name.

- [materials-006] **Sources: `references.md` in the material's folder.** One row per source: `| ID | 名前 | 場所 | 種類 | 元の更新日 | 確かめた日 | 社外 | 使い道 |`. `場所` is the URL, meeting ID, or path as it is, in its own column, so that the script compares and opens it without parsing a name out of it. Add a row with `python3 <deck-practices folder>/scripts/refs.py add <material folder> <URL・会議の ID・パス> [--name <名前>] [--kind <種類>] [--use <使い道>]`. It prints the ID to cite (`S3`), sets `確かめた日` to today, guesses `種類` and `社外` from the URL, and fills `元の更新日` from Drive, GitHub, the file time, or HTTP Last-Modified (「不明」 if none). If the source is already there, it prints that ID and adds nothing. Child agents that read sources add rows the same way (the file is locked while writing, so parallel children do not collide on IDs).
  - `種類`: `公式` (the primary source for the fact: the vendor's docs, the repository, the price list, the authors' paper), `記事` (someone else's summary: blogs, news, search results you then opened), `社内資料`, `会議`, `チャット`, `データ` (local files and tables you computed from), `口頭` (what the user said in the conversation; pass `--kind 口頭` with a target such as 「ユーザー（2026-10-21 の会話）」).
  - `社外`: `可` only for public `公式` and `記事` pages; everything else is `不可` (customer-facing materials cite only public URLs; content-033).
  - `使い道`: the pages or parts used, or why you decided not to use it (keep unused sources, so that you do not search for them again).
- [materials-007] **Facts: the 「出典の表」 section at the end of the outline** (for materials without an outline, the same section in `references.md`). One row per fact the pages state: `| ID | 言うこと | 種類 | 出典 | 確認 |`.
  - `言うこと`: the value or statement as the page says it (「3.2 倍」「東京リージョンで使える」). Write a rounded value in the page's form, with the original if you like (「3.2 倍（3.24）」), because lint compares the digits as written (lint-026).
  - `種類`: `提供` (availability, release stage, region, limits, price, model generation), `計算` (a value computed from other rows), `数字`, `顧客` (the customer's environment and what the customer said), `名前` (proper nouns whose spelling or existence matters), `見立て` (our own framing, inference, or hypothesis; no source, `確認` `-`).
  - `出典`: start with the source IDs, then the place in the source (`S3 の Regions の表`, `S2 の 12:30 ごろ`). For `計算`, start with the rows used and add the formula with the original values (`F4÷F5（48÷15）`).
  - `確認`: `未` (you have not opened the source), `済` (you opened it and it matches), `照合済` (the source-check child opened it and it matches; deck-practices-019). A note may follow in （） (「済（モデルの判定）」).
  - Pages state only the facts in this table. When you need a new one, add the row first (content-064).
  - A file the section names in backticks (for example 「`build/README_data.md` の「数字の一覧」を出典の表とする」) counts as part of the table as a whole, so keep rejected and draft values out of it.
- [materials-008] **Check with `refs.py check <material folder>`.** It reports format errors (exit 1), the rows not yet `照合済` in order of risk (the list the source-check child takes), rows still `未`, product facts whose only source is weak (`記事`, `会議`, `チャット`, `口頭`), and stale rows: `提供` and `数字` rows whose source was checked more than 14 days ago, and sources updated after the date checked (`--fetch` gets the current dates first). After rereading a source, run `refs.py seen <material folder> S3`. The 14 days: model generations, prices, and our own tools changed within two weeks in past corrections.
- Look at `references.md` before searching. Do not keep copies; reread from the URL (for Google Slides, `../deck-build/scripts/gslides.py` also works). It contains internal URLs and materials that cannot go outside the company, so do not put it in the deliverables (reuse.md §2). When the material is finished, add the materials you used to `references_index.md` (the overall index) at the top level.
- Older materials have tables without IDs. `refs.py check` still reads them (rows are numbered `行1`, `行2`, …), and `refs.py add` appends a new table below the old rows. When you change rows of an old 「出典の表」, convert it to the columns above.

Evidence: in 439 corrections (2026-10-01 to 10-21), 37 were factual errors. Review child agents caught 7, all pages that had drifted from a correct table. Facts missing from the table (availability, negations, the customer's environment, frameworks we made up) and 「済」 on a chat, a meeting, or an inference were caught only when someone reopened the URLs before sharing. The table was added to after the first page in 1 of 32 Claude Code sessions, and of 11 high-stakes numbers found nowhere in the folder, 7 were computed values. Rows in the spec's format fell from 21 of 46 ledgers to 2 of 17 over three weeks.
