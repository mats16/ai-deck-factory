# Practices for making materials

Open this first when making or editing materials. It builds on 「いい資料の考え方」 in the root `AGENTS.md` (the user's iron rules and the six things that hold for every material), and holds the workflow and the files to read in each situation. [deck-practices-001] Workspace rules (`AGENTS.md` and the `rules/<skill>/<file>` it points to) take precedence over the plugin's skills. When they conflict, follow the workspace. Points the user has not decided yet are marked 「要判断」 in each file.

## Overall flow

If you are only told 「〇〇の資料を作って」 (even without a folder), proceed in order from 1 in "How to start" below. Decks and documents are the same. For requests after delivery (corrections, questions, comments), do not run all of 8–11; classify them in "Requests after delivery" below and run only as far as written there. The human answers only the four questions in 5 (and the outline when `plan_reviewer` is `"human"`) and the list of questions for the user in 10. Once the structure is agreed in 5, run through to 10 without waiting for the user (deck-practices-006 in "Working practices" below).

| Step | What to do | Why it is there (what it prevents) | Who | Condition to move on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 Preparation | Decide the folder; read past materials, internal materials, and customer sources | Do not research the same thing twice. Align with internal wording and messages. Do not use numbers without a source | Parent (heavy reading by child agents) | The sources you read are rows of `references.md` (`refs.py add`) |
| 5 Agreeing on the structure | Confirm the four questions, choose a theme, write the outline | If you get the audience or the question wrong, every later step must be redone. Only here does a human check | Human and parent | The four answers and the outline (for 10 pages or fewer, the four answers and the plan) |
| 5 Plan review | A child agent without the conversation compares the request with the outline | Finds gaps and omissions against the request that the maker does not notice, before building (after building, many pages must be redone) | Child agent (a human for ②) | The findings are reflected in the outline |
| 6 Three representative pages | Build three pages of different types, have a child agent review them, and turn fixes to type and writing into the 「書き方の決め」 section | Defects in the frame and in components (how they look, size) and in writing spread to every page. Find them on three pages before writing the rest | Parent builds, child agent reviews | 「書き方の決め」 is written and applied to the three pages' JSON (the three are not rebuilt) |
| 7 Remaining pages | Write the rest, copying only the frame from the three pages (visual-049). Split among child agents if there are many | Shortens writing time. Variation from splitting among child agents is held down by the decisions in 6 and the search in 8 | Parent or child agent | 0 `要修正` from `slides.py lint` on every page |
| 8 Build the whole | Apply 「書き方の決め」 to every page, build, and check with the warnings, a JSON search, and the contact sheet | Missed applications, variation between child agents, size and position drift across pages | Parent | 0 `要修正` from `check --all`; variation fixed |
| 9 `/deck-review` | A child agent without the conversation looks at the whole. Fix, and run rounds (ask, verify the findings, fix, rebuild and check) | Cross-page defects that page-by-page checks miss, and defects the maker overlooks | Child agent and parent | 0 findings to fix, or the round limit |
| 10 Deliver | Return the URL, the findings table, and the list of questions for the user in one message | A human decides, all at once, on what was run through without asking | Parent | Reported |
| 11 Clean up | Fill in the rule ledger and the log; delete working copies | Do not miss routing corrections into rules. Do not leave working copies | Parent | 0 ledger entries whose destination is 「未」 |

- **Decks of 10 pages or fewer:** skip 6, write the whole deck in 7, and continue from 8. Run the plan review regardless of page count.
- **Documents:** there is no step 6 and no splitting the writing among child agents in 7 (the parent writes the single doc.md). For building and checking, see `../doc-build/SKILL.md`; the check in 8 is `docs.py check`.
- **The layers of check tools** (`slides.py lint` after every edit, `build` and `check` at checkpoints, a whole-deck pass once per checkpoint) are in deck-build-005 in `../deck-build/SKILL.md`. Where in the flow to build is written in each step below.

## How to start

### 1. Decide the folder

If none is given, decide it from the request and tell the user in the first reply (ask only when you cannot tell whether the material is for a customer or internal). Customer-facing: `Customers/<customer>/<content>_<date>/`; internal: `Internal/<content>_<date>/`; research that produces no material: `Research/` (「調べもの」 in AGENTS.md). The date is the meeting or submission date; if unknown, this month. Do not create files directly under the workspace root (「フォルダの置き方」 in AGENTS.md). If the folder already exists, read its contents first (`構成案*.md`, `社内メモ_*.md`, `references.md`, `build/`).

- [deck-practices-002] **Folder names:** one folder per material. Name it `<content>_<date>` in lowercase English (e.g. `Customers/<customer>/sample_briefing_202610/`). Create a folder even for a single-file material. Revisions of the same material go in the same folder. Things remade for every regular meeting (schedules, etc.) get a new folder each time (e.g. `schedule_20261007/`, `schedule_20260930/`). Internal materials (study sessions, meetups, enablement) that do not belong to a customer go in `Internal/`.
- **Folder contents:** do not keep decks and documents locally; build them as Google Slides and Docs on Drive (the URL goes in the outline). Whether to delete `build/` is decided in "What to keep" below.
  ```
  構成案.md               … if any
  社内メモ_<content>.md   … things not shown to the customer (speaking order, 要確認 items, personal names, internal circumstances)
  build/                  … source of truth for the content (decks: deck.json and one file per page p<number>.json [older materials: pages.py]; documents: doc.md). Images used only by this material go in build/assets/
  references.md           … ledger of the sources read while making it (one row per source with an S<number> ID; `refs.py add`; format: "Ledger" in materials.md)
  ```

### 2. Read past materials for the same customer

Other materials in the customer folder and `社内メモ_*.md` describe the counterpart and the history; the customer's `memory.md` holds preferences that apply across materials (the hook shows it after you open `構成案*.md` etc.; "Customer memory" in `rules.md`). Also open the customer's `profile.md` (business, organization, technical setup, engineers' preferences). If it is missing or stale ("Going stale" in `customer-profile`), create or refresh it with the `customer-profile` skill before the outline.

### 3. Find internal materials

Search `references_index.md` first. Then find internal decks, documents, and chat discussions on the same theme, and decide what to reuse and which messages to align with ([materials.md](materials.md); how to reuse: [reuse.md](reuse.md)).

### 4. Gather customer information

From meeting records and internal chat (`[sources]` in `factory.toml`), pick up the customer's interests, the history, and what was discussed so far ([materials.md](materials.md)). Meetings cited before are in `references_index.md`. Add every source you read in 3 and 4 to `references.md` with `refs.py add`, and note each fact you may use with the source ID and the place in it (「S3 の Regions の表」) ("Ledger" in materials.md). Child agents that read sources add their rows the same way and return facts with the IDs.

### 5. Agree on the structure

[deck-practices-003] **Agree on the structure.** For every material, before starting, confirm with the user the material type, the audience, the audience's question, and what the audience can do after reading (「いい資料の考え方」 in AGENTS.md). Confirm even if you could write them from the request. Open [content.md](content.md) before you confirm them, because its §1 and content-039 decide what to ask for each material type (such as the builder's intent for materials that introduce a mechanism). The review after the plan (the outline) is decided follows `[review] plan_reviewer` in `factory.toml`. `"ai"` means ① AI reviews and you go on to build; `"human"` means ② a human reviews (if unset, ①). If the user names the other one for this material, use that. Write which one you chose in the outline's premises. The default is ①, and the human checks only the first four questions (the three representative pages, the check on every build, and `/deck-review` are also AI). ② is kept because the transition is still in progress. Materials that explain this mechanism should say so too. For decks over 10 pages, long documents, or when the counterpart or the purpose is unclear, write `構成案.md` before making the material (otherwise pass the four answers and the plan to the review). Write the key points per page for decks and per chapter for documents, and list reused internal materials in 「流用元」 at the end with URL and page number. When revising, bump the number, as in `構成案_v2.md`.

- **Choose a theme.** Themes are the workspace's theme files (`themes/slides/<name>.json`; built-in: visual and company) and document theme files (`themes/docs/<name>.json`); choose by their `description` (modeled on how Agent Skills selects a skill). `slides.py themes` (for documents, `docs.py themes`) prints the name, whether it is the default, whether it has a template, and the `description`. Match the `description` against the material type, the audience, and any company color requirement, and write 「テーマ: <名前>（<選んだ理由>）」 in the outline's premises. The default theme is for when the `description` does not decide it. When the choice is not decided, conflicts with the user's instruction, or you want a theme that has no template, ask together with the structure questions. For decks, write it in `theme` in `build/deck.json` (`new` creates the deck with that theme, and `lint` and `dry` check with it; `../deck-build/slides.md` §3). For documents, write `theme:` at the top of doc.md (`theme: standard` if left as default). If there are two or more themes and you run `new` without choosing, it prints the list and stops.
- **Write three things for each page in the outline.** The heading (a short noun phrase and one sentence it can claim), the page type (parallel, contrast, progression, structure, etc.; visual.md §6), and the evidence with its source. Choose the type page by page from what the page says, not by copying the representative pages or a past deck (visual-048, 049). [deck-practices-012] For pages with an assertive heading (the 2–3 pages that ask for a decision or conclude with a number), write 「言い切り」 in the type column (content.md §4). Write every fact the pages will state (numbers, computed values, availability, the customer's environment, proper nouns, and our own `見立て`) as rows of the 「出典の表」 at the end of the outline (columns and kinds: "Ledger" in materials.md; content-064). Run `refs.py check <material folder>` and fix its format errors. lint reports numbers on pages that are not in the table, and the review child agent in 9 compares the table with the numbers on the slides (aspect 2 in `../deck-review/prompt_slides.md`. Do not let a model that looks at images judge numeric errors; compare them with the source text. 研究 1-10).
- **Read only the headings and the claims in order, and check that the story holds.** When the outline is written, the parent reads them and also has the plan-review child agent look. After building, the review child agent in 9 reads the list of headings and leads that the lint in `gslides.py check` prints (aspect 1. 中身 1-1、研究 1-1・1-2).
- [deck-practices-019] **Check the facts against the sources in the background.** Right after writing the 「出典の表」, start a source-check child agent with pass 1 of `../deck-review/prompt_sources.md`, in the same message as the plan-review child. Start pass 2 in 9, together with round 1 of `/deck-review`. Do not wait for either (deck-practices-013): keep writing and building, and take the results in at the next point where you put in fixes (the three-page fixes in 6, a review round's fixes in 9). For each row: `合う` → set `確認` to `照合済`; `違う` or `言い過ぎ` → fix the row and the same value on every page, in `authorNotes`, and in any notes (search `build/*.json`), and list it on delivery, but if the fix breaks a heading's claim or the page's point, leave the page and ask in 10 (deck-practices-010); `確かめられない` → ask in 10, and in customer-facing materials do not state it as fact. If the child lists items under 「見ていない」, start another child on those, also in the background. If a pass has not returned by delivery, deliver without waiting, list its rows as 「照合中」 in the report, and put the results in as a content/structure fix when it returns. The findings file is `/tmp/sources_<material folder name>_<1 or 2>.md`; write the path and the child's task ID in the outline, so that they survive summarizing. Evidence: "Ledger" in materials.md (the maker's 「済」 and the review child agents missed errors whose source was weak or that were not in the table at all).
- **Plan review:** for every material, run it the way `plan_reviewer` says.
  - **② Human:** show the outline (for materials without an outline, the four answers and the plan) and get confirmation.
  - [deck-practices-004] **① AI:** give a new subagent without the conversation the user's original request (you may format and organize it, but do not change its meaning), the material's direction (`構成案*.md`, or the four answers and the plan if there is none), and the paths to `社内メモ_*.md` and `references.md` (for it to open when it wants to check sources). Do not pass the conversation or the raw sources (minutes, transcripts, etc.). They pull it toward the maker's thinking and weaken the point of a second pair of eyes. Before passing, write everything decided in the conversation and every rejected idea into the outline or the internal memo. How to ask: "Ask a child agent" in "Working practices" below. The request text: "Read the original request and the direction, check whether the direction answers the request, and list only the gaps and omissions. Check: whether the audience, the question, and what the audience can do after reading match the request; what is in the request but missing from the direction, and what was added though not in the request; whether the story holds when reading only the headings in order; and whether it departs from the six points of 「いい資料の考え方」 in AGENTS.md (「主役は聞き手」「問いを先に」「煽らない」「新しい見方」「公平」「見解」)." While waiting, start writing the page JSON of the three representative pages (for decks that skip them, the whole) (deck-practices-013). Verify the findings yourself; for those you may fix, fix the outline and go straight on to 6. For those that change the audience, the question, or the claim, change nothing, proceed, and put them in the list of questions for the user in 10. Record the findings received and the fixes made at the end of the outline.
- After the plan review, call the context-summarizing tool if you can (deck-practices-005).

### 6. Three representative pages (decks over 10 pages)

[deck-practices-007] **Three representative pages:** for decks over 10 pages, after the plan review, build only three body pages of different types (a diagram, a table or code, the page with the most text or elements, etc.) (write the page JSON, list only those three in `pages` of `deck.json`, and run `slides.py new`), and check them yourself before building the whole. Do not include the cover or section dividers. They are fixed types, so looking at them decides nothing. Do not ask for approval and do not wait. Share the URL as soon as it is created (deck-build-003 in `../deck-build/SKILL.md`; whether to look is up to the user). How to check:
   1. Bring the lint `要修正` of `gslides.py check <URL>` to 0 and open the three images yourself.
   2. [deck-practices-008] Ask a review child agent ("Ask a child agent" in "Working practices" below) to review the three pages. Write the request in the page-reviewer form of `../deck-review/prompt_slides.md` (aspects 2 and 5), and add at the top of "What to check": "List first the defects in the frame and in components that would repeat on every page using them (diagrams, tables, amount and size of text, margins; visual-049)." For a rebuild or a port, also pass the images of the same pages in the original version and have it compare them (step 2 of deck-review). While waiting, write the remaining pages in JSON (7).
   3. Classify the findings with "What to fix" in deck-review; fix those to fix and send those to ask to 10. Divide the fixes into three by how far they spread. Do not rebuild the three pages and do not rerun 1 and 2; go on to 7.
      - Fixes by type (component choice, arrangement, font size, and color that are the same on pages of the same type; table row height; tool constraints): write them one per line in the 「書き方の決め」 section of the outline, in one of two groups: 「デッキで共通」 for the frame that every page shares (positions of the heading, lead, and source line, text sizes, colors, how a component looks, names; visual-049 in visual.md), and 「形ごと」 for lines that hold only on pages of one form, each starting with the form's name (「表:」「流れ図:」). Do not write which form a page uses there; that is the outline's type column, because a line like 「本文は 3 枚のカード」 spreads one form to every page. Apply them to the three pages' JSON before writing the remaining pages (before asking child agents) (they spread to every page, so always fix them before building the whole. Use `grep` to find the same component). For a fix that changes how something is drawn and that you are not sure works, run `slides.py lint` on that page; only when lint cannot tell, build the three pages and look at the images.
      - Fixes in writing (line break positions, characters per line in cards, the scope and number of headings, limiting words such as 「だけ」「すべて」, the first use of rephrased terms and abbreviations): also write them one per line in 「書き方の決め」 and apply them to the three pages before writing the rest.
      - Fixes for that page only: fix that page while writing the remaining pages in 7 (while waiting, if you asked child agents). Do not make type-level fixes while waiting (child agents copy the frame from the three pages).
      - The fixes to the three pages are built together with the whole in 8, and checked by `gslides.py check --all` on the whole and by `/deck-review`.
   4. [deck-practices-009] Add one section, in the form at its top, to the log (the workspace's `Research/deck_research/log/20261009_rep3_selection.md`. It is a per-material log, so write it in the workspace, and route back only what turns out to affect rules, following "Rule layers" in [rules.md](rules.md). Keep this up for the next 10 materials, to see whether the application method in 8 and the card-height and last-line warnings added on 2026-10-13 worked) with: the three pages chosen and why, the findings and the fixes, and the number of 「書き方の決め」 lines and the number of pages they were applied to. Also write it in the report in 10, in up to three lines. This log is used to review the selection guide and the application method in 8.
- After writing the log, call the context-summarizing tool if you can (deck-practices-005).

Evidence: previously we passed the URL and waited for approval of the three pages, but they were approved without being looked at (in 12 past approvals, 0 replies touched the content of the three pages; `Research/deck_rep3_approval_sessions_20261009.md`). We compare with the original version in a rebuild because rounded corners and icons once decreased from the original and both the human approval and the review missed it. The three pages are not rebuilt because, in 48 logged materials, a second check newly found defects spreading to every page in only 2 materials, 3 cases (all side effects of line-break fixes), and the second check took 10–19 minutes per material (`20261009_rep3_selection.md`, counted on 2026-10-17). The context after the three pages is 230k–340k tokens at the median, and the building steps alone add about 500k (2026-10-10; `Research/pi_compaction_deck_workflow_20261010/README.md`). AI 実践 1-3.

### 7. Write the remaining pages

For decks of 10 pages or fewer, write the whole deck here. For decks that went through the three representative pages, write the remaining pages in `build/p<number>.json`, copying only the frame from the three pages' JSON and choosing each page's form from what it says (visual-048, 049; slides-034 in `../deck-build/slides.md`). Keep only the three pages listed in `pages` of `deck.json`; just write the rest to files (they are listed when building the whole in 8). Run `slides.py lint` on the pages you wrote and bring `要修正` to 0. For each `出典の表にない数字`, add the row to the 「出典の表」 with its source first, or use the table's value (content-064).

- [deck-practices-016] **Split the remaining pages among child agents (decks only).** You may have child agents write the remaining pages in parallel, with the parent listing and building them. The parent decides whether and how to split. Do not split decks of 30 pages or fewer, or when the remainder is not enough for two child agents (the child agents' startup time cancels the gain). Split by chapter (section divider) or by groups of a few pages in the outline, and give pages of the same type or the same diagram to the same child agent. The biggest risk is variation in writing between child agents; hold it down with item 1 below and the checks in 8. Documents are not split, because the parent writes the single doc.md.
  1. Before asking, write out all of the 「書き方の決め」 fixed in step 3 of 6 in the outline, apply them to the three pages' JSON, and add the numbers and phrasings that must be the same across pages (names, the page where an abbreviation first appears). For things that appear on pages of two or more assignees (the same tool, mechanism, or tier), decide down to the one-line explanation and write it. Child agents copy only the frame from the three representative pages' JSON (visual-049) and do not rewrite them. In a split deck, what the parent writes while waiting for the three-page review is the draft of 「書き方の決め」 and the request texts; the child agents write the remaining pages.
  2. Ask one child agent per assignment with `../deck-build/prompt_pages.md`. The child agent writes its pages in JSON, one file per page (`build/p<number>.json`), runs `slides.py lint` on a list `build/pages_<owner>.json` without building, and returns. Have it write anything that changes claims, numbers, or order, and anything the rules do not decide, as the outline says, and return it to the parent.
  3. When they return, list the child agents' pages in `pages` of `deck.json` in outline order (slides-031 in `../deck-build/slides.md`) and go on to 8. Of what the child agents returned, send the items to ask to 10, and write the forms they changed into the outline's type column. In the three-representative-pages log, write how you split (number of child agents and their pages).

Evidence: the step where the parent writes alone in order ("write the whole and build") takes a median of 17 minutes per material (14 materials); splitting among child agents is expected to save about 10 minutes on decks over 30 pages (estimate; 「候補と見込み」 ⑤ in the outline in the workspace's `Internal/deck_speed_202610/`). In a 13-page trial, all 4 cases of variation across writers were in things that appeared on pages of two or more assignees.

### 8. Build and check the whole deck

[deck-practices-011] **Before building the whole, apply what was fixed in 6 to the remaining pages.** After writing the remaining pages in JSON, go through 「書き方の決め」 line by line and fix them against the headings, cards, and table cells of every page (for headings, recount the tables and cards in the body; for rephrased terms and abbreviations, search `build/*.json`). Also apply the component fixes made on the three pages (e.g. omitting a card's `h` and sizing it from its content) to the same components on the remaining pages. If you build without applying them, the defects found on the three pages appear unchanged on the remaining pages.

After applying them, list all pages in `pages` of `deck.json` and build once to the same URL (`slides.py build`; for decks that skipped 6, create the deck here with `slides.py new`). Then run the whole-deck layer of checks (deck-build-005 in `../deck-build/SKILL.md`).

- Look at each build warning (`slides.py` prints them to stderr: a last line of 2 characters or fewer, content that does not fit in `h`, etc.) one by one.
- Bring the `要修正` of `gslides.py check <URL> --all` to 0 and look at each `要確認` one by one.
- For decks written by split child agents, find and fix variation between child agents (size and position of same-type components, characters in cards, phrasing) with the warnings, a search of `build/*.json`, and the contact sheet.
- In the contact sheet of all pages (`sheet.py`), check the size and position of same-type components, the density, and the color.
- Do not open each page at full size. The full-size images are viewed by the review child agent in round 1 of 9. The parent opens at full size only the pages flagged above (the case in deck-build-005 for building the whole for the first time and going straight into `/deck-review`).

Evidence: in 21 logged materials, about 60% of the every-page defects found after building the whole, within the range visible on the three pages, were cases where the fixes on the three pages had not been applied to the remaining pages (the application method in 8 and the card-height and last-line warnings were added on 2026-10-13).

### 9. Review the whole with `/deck-review` before delivering

Run it by the steps in `../deck-review/SKILL.md`, and start pass 2 of the source check together with round 1 (deck-practices-019). Another subagent's eyes and the contact sheet of all pages also catch cross-page defects. Run up to the `[review] deck_review_max_rounds` rounds in `factory.toml` (3 rounds if unset); if fixing increases the defects, revert. After fixing, rebuild decks and documents to the same URL ("Deliverables" in `../deck-build/SKILL.md`). For decks that went through the three representative pages, write the every-page defects found in the review under 「あと」 in the same section of the three-representative-pages log (if something remained even though it was applied in 8, say so).

### 10. Deliver

[deck-practices-020] **Before the report, check the ledger.** Run `refs.py check <material folder> --fetch`. Reread the sources it lists as stale or updated after the date checked, fix what changed (rows and pages), and run `refs.py seen`. Resolve the rows still `未`: open the source, or take the fact off the pages and ask. Also run it when a request after delivery changes the facts of a material checked more than 14 days ago. Stale sources caused wrong model generations, prices, and descriptions of our own tools in past corrections.

[deck-practices-022] **When the request also asks for speaker notes or a video, go on to them right after the deck is finished, without stopping.** Run in this order: finish the deck (9 and deck-practices-020, which can still change pages) → write the notes for the whole deck by `../slides-speaker-notes/SKILL.md` → make the video by `../vids-beta/SKILL.md`, only if a video was asked for → deliver everything in one report. Keep the order, because the notes are written from the finished pages and the video reads the notes. A request for a video alone also runs the notes first. Do not report or wait for the user between the steps. If a step would ask the user (such as moving content from the notes to the pages in step 2 of vids-beta), skip that part and put the question in the final report. Do not ask about the video: it uses a Conversational voice unless the request names a voice or avatar scenes (step 1 of vids-beta).

Put the final report in one message. Include:

- The Drive URLs (the material and anything else you made)
- The `/deck-review` findings table and the count per round (step 7 of `../deck-review/SKILL.md`)
- The list of questions for the user: the items set to 「聞く」 along the way (plan-review findings that change the audience, the question, or the claim; proposals that change claims, numbers, or order; proposals to add, delete, or split pages; things the rules do not decide; what child agents returned). Pages split because they did not fit are also asked about here (deck-practices-010)
- The key points of the three-representative-pages log (up to 3 lines; deck-practices-009)
- Which correction was routed to which rule (「修正の指示」 in AGENTS.md)
- What the source check changed, and the rows still 「照合中」 or `確かめられない` (deck-practices-019)
- If notes or a video were asked for: what step 7 of `../slides-speaker-notes/SKILL.md` lists, and the Vids URL (deck-practices-022)

If `[feedback.google_comments] watch_hours` in `factory.toml` is greater than 0, start watching comments after delivery (deck-comments-004 in `../deck-comments/SKILL.md`). If it is 0 or unset, do not watch.

Check that no findings for this material remain with the destination 「未」 (`ledger.py list --deck <material folder> --open`; so that if a correction drops out of a conversation summary, it can be picked up again from the ledger), and that 「あと」 in the three-representative-pages log is filled in ([rules.md](rules.md)). Add the materials you used to `references_index.md`. Do not leave PDFs or images used for checking in the folder ("What to keep" below).

## Working practices (every step)

- [deck-practices-006] **Once the structure is agreed in 5, run through to delivery in 10 without waiting for the user.** For items that become 「聞く」 along the way (proposals that change claims, numbers, or order; proposals to add, delete, or split pages; things the rules do not decide), leave that page unchanged, move on, and collect them in the list of questions for the user in 10 (only splitting when something does not fit is applied first; deck-practices-010 below). Before you start writing, open the files in the deck or document row of the table in "What to read when" below.
- [deck-practices-010] **Do not silently change confirmed claims, numbers, or order.** When something does not fit, fit it by rephrasing and diagramming before making the text smaller. If splitting makes it fit, split it, build, and ask in 10 (do not deliver it overflowing). If it does not fit without cutting, leave the page unchanged, move on, and ask about the cut in 10 (AI 実践 1-3).
- **What you edit is the page JSON (for documents, doc.md) and the outline.** For decks, write in `build/deck.json` and one file per page `build/p<number>.json` (slides-034 in `../deck-build/slides.md`), and build to the Drive location with `slides.py` of `deck-build`. Use the JSON components (`slides.py schema`); do not copy from previous materials. Page types (cards, comparisons, number highlights, conclusion bands) are arrangements of the same components; do not make a new component for one page (AI 実践 1-9、研究 1-6). Choose each page's arrangement from what it says (visual-048, 049). When you fix something in the Google Slides UI, put the same fix into the page JSON too (rebuilding erases edits made in the UI). For materials previously made with pages.py, edit pages.py (`../deck-build/slides.md` §2). For documents, write only the content in markdown in `build/doc.md` and build with `docs.py` of `doc-build` (`../doc-build/SKILL.md` §1).
- **Check locally after every edit; build and look at images at checkpoints.** After every edit, bring the `要修正` of `slides.py lint` to 0; run `slides.py build` and `gslides.py check` only at checkpoints (the three pages in 6, building the whole in 8, after putting in a round's fixes in 9). What each layer checks and who looks at the images: deck-build-005 in `../deck-build/SKILL.md`. For documents, `docs.py check` (§5 of `../doc-build/SKILL.md`). Bring the lint `要修正` to 0; look at each `要確認` and either fix it or be able to say why it stays.
- **When the user gives a correction,** record it and fix it as 「修正の指示」 in AGENTS.md says, and route it to a rule on the spot (destination and how to verify: [rules.md](rules.md)).
- **Ask a child agent.** The plan review, the three-page review, page-writing child agents, and `/deck-review` child agents are all asked of a new child agent without the conversation, with the same model as the main agent stated explicitly (how to start one in each environment: "How to ask" in `../deck-review/SKILL.md`; 「基本方針」 in AGENTS.md). The parent takes the images; do not have child agents run `gslides.py check` (deck-build-005).
  - [deck-practices-013] **After asking a child agent, do work that does not depend on its result while waiting.** Run child agents in the background (Claude Code: background child agents; T3 Code: delegation with `async`), and receive completion by notification or with that environment's wait tool. In environments where the child-agent tool does not return until the child is done (e.g. pi's subagent extension without T3), finish the waiting-time work first, then ask, and call several child agents at once in one message. Do not watch files a child agent writes with `sleep`. When writing page JSON (for documents, doc.md) while waiting, first open the files in the deck or document row of "What to read when".
    Do not do work that depends on the child agent's result (fixing findings) first. If something you wrote while waiting must be rewritten because of the findings, fold it into the next fix. Do not rebuild the material a child agent is looking at while waiting, and do not run `gslides.py check` or `docs.py check`. Do not rewrite files in the folders a child agent opens (`/tmp/slides_<ID>/` etc.) either (the images the child agent sees would change). While waiting for fix child agents (deck-review-011 in `../deck-review/SKILL.md`), as 011 says, do not write the files of the child agent's pages; put the parent's share into other files. Put fixes you find yourself into the page JSON (for documents, doc.md) and build them together with the fixes for the findings. Wait only when there is nothing left to do. For T3 Code delegation, wait with `t3_thread_wait` (threadId and runId are the `childThreadId` and `childRunId` returned by `delegate_task`; `timeoutMs` is 270000). It returns as soon as the child agent finishes; if it does not finish, it returns `timedOut` after 270 seconds, so call it again (calling again refreshes the prompt cache before its 5 minutes run out). With several child agents, wait for one at a time in order (if you call the waits at once in one message, the results of child agents that finished early do not arrive until the slowest one returns; in the sandbox on 2026-10-18 this caused a 4-minute delay). When it returns, read the result with `task_status` (once read, the same completion notification does not arrive later). For Claude Code background child agents, end the turn and wait for the notification. In Codex and in environments where ending the turn ends the conversation (`claude -p`, etc.), wait with the tool for waiting on child agents. pi's extension stops a `sleep` of 30 seconds or more (Claude Code stops a Bash `sleep` itself).
    What to do while waiting:
    - Research child agent: the skeleton of the outline (the sections the research results do not change)
    - Plan-review child agent (5): the page JSON of the three representative pages (for decks that skip them, the whole)
    - Three-page review child agent (6): the JSON of the remaining pages (for split decks, the draft of 「書き方の決め」 and the request texts; deck-practices-016 in 7)
    - Child agents writing the remaining pages (7): fixes to the three representative pages that affect only that page (step 3 of 6)
    - Source-check child agents (5, 9; deck-practices-019): nothing waits for them; continue the step you are on
    - `/deck-review` child agents (9): the records up to the previous round (the findings table, 「あと」 in the three-representative-pages log), `references.md`, the lint `要確認`, and fixes you found yourself
- [deck-practices-005] **If a context-summarizing tool is available, call it at step boundaries.** If there is no such tool (e.g. pi's `compact_context`), proceed without calling it. Do not stop to ask the user to summarize (it was recommended but not used). There are four boundaries: after the plan review (before 6), after writing the three-representative-pages log (before building the whole), after checking the fixed pages in each `/deck-review` round (deck-review-013 in `../deck-review/SKILL.md`), and before delivering to the user (10, and when delivering a request after delivery; deck-practices-017). After delivery there is often a gap until the next request, and the longer the gap, the more context is rewritten (see "Evidence" below). Before calling, write out the decisions, the rejected ideas and why, the sources, the `要確認` items, the list of questions for the user, and the next steps into `構成案*.md` and `社内メモ_*.md` (corrections go in the rule ledger; review findings in deck-review's `$D/review.md`). Write them even without the tool (so they survive automatic summarization). Only the beginning of the tool's result survives the summary (pi: 2,000 characters). If the tool accepts follow-up instructions (`next` of `compact_context`), write where in the steps to resume and which files to re-read (deck-practices-015 below).
  - [deck-practices-015] **After summarizing, re-read only the files the next step needs.** The working folder's `構成案*.md` and `社内メモ_*.md`, the material's findings (`ledger.py list --deck <material folder>`), the customer's `memory.md` and `profile.md` for customer-facing materials, and the section of the step you continue with. Open `references.md` when verifying sources. Do not re-read skill files (`slides.md`, `visual.md`, etc.; about 20,000 characters each) or the page JSON in full; find the section you will use next and the pages to fix with `grep -n`, and open those.

Evidence:
- Waiting: in 23 pi logs, out of a median of 146 minutes per material, the parent sat waiting for child agents for a median of 22.5 minutes. In one 23-page material, the first 135 minutes had 6 waits totaling 30 minutes, with the parent either watching the child agents' files with `sleep` or stopped waiting for a notification (`Internal/deck_speed_202610/社内メモ_計測の結果.md`, p.8 and p.10 of the material in the same folder). In one 25-page material on 2026-10-16 (98 minutes), the parent waited 6 times with `sleep 270` and `task_status`, and it took about 11 minutes in total between the child agents finishing and the parent noticing. In a 14-page sandbox run on 2026-10-18 (50 minutes) using `t3_thread_wait`, all 4 waits returned within 1 second of the child agent finishing.
- Summarizing: one call takes 9.6 seconds at 100k–200k, 11.7 seconds at 200k–300k, and 17.0 seconds at 300k–400k. Calls at boundaries were mostly after the plan review and before building the whole; they were rarely made in `/deck-review` rounds, and the automatic summary at 400k ran instead. Context that was 80k–90k right after a summary went back to a median of 210k after 30 calls because of bulk re-reading (2026-10-14; 23 pi logs; `Internal/deck_speed_202610/社内メモ_計測の結果.md`, p.7 and p.12 of the slides in the same folder; the earlier study is `Research/pi_compaction_deck_workflow_20261010/README.md`). Summarizing before delivery: of 67 revision requests after delivery, 41 started more than 5 minutes after the previous reply (the prompt cache expires), and the first call rewrote a median of 248k tokens. This rewriting was 24% of the cost of the 67. Summarizing before delivering is expected to save $1.0–1.2 per request (estimate; 2026-10-19; the workspace's `Internal/deck_revision_speed_20261019/`).
- Not waiting for the user: in 12 past approvals, 0 replies touched the content of the three pages (`Research/deck_rep3_approval_sessions_20261009.md`).

## Requests after delivery

[deck-practices-017] **For a request after delivery, first classify its type, then run only as far as that type's row says.** Requests after delivery are not only page fixes. There are also questions about the content (「この数字はどこから」「なぜこの順か」), research to add, and changes of audience. Running steps 8–11 for every one of them means building the whole and running `/deck-review` even for a wording fix. If asked in a new conversation, first read the history as in 1 and 2 (the outline, the internal memo, and the material's findings via `ledger.py list --deck <material folder>`). If one message mixes types, answer the questions first and run the fixes in the heavier row. Separate small fixes from content fixes by whether the page's point (what its heading claims), the numbers, or the number and order of pages change. A replacement where the user gave the exact wording follows the instruction even if the point changes, so treat it as a small fix if the page count and order do not change (numbers are a content/structure fix even when the user gives the value, so that you verify the source and apply it to the same number on other pages); if it is a heading, the parent re-reads the heading flow in `deck.md`. If unsure how to classify, choose the heavier one.

| Type | Examples | What to do | Build and check | Review child agent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Question | The source of this number, why this order, what this term refers to, whether there are other options | Verify with the outline's source table, `references.md`, and the sources, and answer. Do not change the material. If the answer reveals an error in the material (a number, a source, an overstatement), attach a proposed fix and ask whether to fix it (deck-practices-010) | No | Do not start one |
| Small fix | Wording, line breaks, color, size, arrangement that does not change a diagram's meaning, unifying notation, a replacement with the wording specified | Fix that page's JSON (for documents, doc.md) | lint → build once → the parent looks at the images of the changed pages (`changed.json`). If there are many, look at the contact sheet and open only the flagged pages at full size | Do not start one |
| Content/structure fix | Change a claim or a number, change a diagram's meaning (the direction of an arrow, etc.), add, delete, split, or reorder a point in a summary or list, an example, or a page, research and add, adjust the tone of the wording to the audience | Write added pages with the frame of nearby pages and 「書き方の決め」, choosing the form from what the page says (visual-048); if no location is given, decide it, insert the page, and mention it on delivery. If sources are needed, gather them as in 3 and 4 with `refs.py add`, and add the facts to the 「出典の表」 before the pages. If rows were added or changed, start pass 2 of the source check on the changed pages in the background (deck-practices-019). Write the changed policy in the outline | lint → build once → images of the changed pages. If the page count or order changed, also `check --all` and the contact sheet | One round on only the changed pages. If the heading flow or the order changed, also one whole-deck child agent (aspects 1 and 3); if numbers changed, also show the whole-deck child agent the cross-page numbers of aspect 2 (totals, ratios, summaries) |
| Major fix | Rearrange the material's storyline (the opening summary and the order of headings) (「結論から先に」「事例から入る」), or change more than half of the pages | If something missing from the request (which case study, what to recommend) changes the storyline, ask first. If sources are needed, gather them as in 3 and 4. Rewrite the outline as `構成案_v2.md`, run the plan review as `plan_reviewer` says (deck-practices-004 in 5; pass the original request plus this message), then run 6–9 in the same order as when making the material, and clean up in 11 (the three representative pages in 6 may be skipped if the page types do not change). Deliver in the form of 10 plus what was checked as in "Deliver" below. Usable parts of the current pages may be kept | The same 8 as when making | Run `/deck-review` in 9 up to the round limit, as when making |
| Premise fix | Change the audience, the question, the material type, or what the audience can do after reading from what the outline says | Go back to 5, confirm the changed answers with the user (only here, wait for the reply), then run it as a major fix. Being told 「合っていない」 while the outline's audience stays the same is a content/structure fix or a major fix | The same 8 as when making | `/deck-review` in 9 |

- **Telling questions from fixes.** Statements of a problem (「分かりにくい」「〜に寄り過ぎている」「〜は要らない」「逆向きでは」) are treated as fix requests. If you verify and the current version is correct, do not fix it; answer with the reason. Statements asking for an opinion (「この順でいい？」) get an answer and a proposed fix, and you ask whether to fix. Items where you only answered a question are not written to the rule ledger. Items written to the ledger as fix requests but not fixed after verifying get `ledger.py route <c-ID> 戻さない`.
- [deck-practices-018] **In a conversation making or editing a material, when told 「図解して」「図にして」, add a page to that material and draw it** (a content/structure fix). Do not settle for HTML in the chat or a diagram in the reply. Ask only when what to diagram is not decided (once a chat diagram was returned, and the user restated that they meant adding it to the slides).
- **Apply changed terms and numbers to other pages too.** For every type of fix, search `build/*.json` (for documents, `build/doc.md`) for the changed terms and numbers, fix them also on pages and summaries that were not mentioned, and list the extra pages on delivery.
- **The rules for making still apply after delivery.** Do not silently change confirmed claims, numbers, or order; when something does not fit, consider rephrasing, diagramming, and splitting in that order, and if you split, ask on delivery (deck-practices-010).
- **Build once.** Put in all the fixes and bring the `要修正` of `slides.py lint` to 0 before building (the checkpoint in deck-build-005). Only if the images show a fix is needed, fix it and build once more.
- **For documents,** read "changed pages" in the table as "changed sections". After building, check with `docs.py check`; page breaks move, so view the images of all pages in the contact sheet and open the pages of the changed sections at full size (§5 of `../doc-build/SKILL.md`; there is no `changed.json` or `check --all`). Give the review child agent the full text of `text.md` and the headings of the changed sections.
- **When asking a review child agent,** use the page-reviewer form of `../deck-review/prompt_slides.md` and pass the `changed.json` entries of the changed pages (including added pages), the full `deck.md`, and the outline. If the page count or order changes, `changed.json` covers all pages, so note the changed pages' numbers as numbered after building and extract only those entries. What the page reviewer returns as 「全体へ」 goes to the whole-deck child agent if you started one; if not, the parent checks it in `deck.md` (for documents, use `prompt_doc.md`, as in the row above) (how to ask: "How to ask" in `../deck-review/SKILL.md`). Add 「直したこと」 (one line per page) to the request. Verify the findings by step 4 of deck-review, and fix them after classifying them with "What to fix". The child agents for a content/structure fix (the changed-page reviewer, and the whole-deck child agent if started) stop after one round; after fixing, the parent looks at the images and delivers (do not ask them again). `/deck-review` for major and premise fixes runs up to the round limit as in 9. Write the table to `$D/review.md` and import it with `ledger.py review` (step 7 of deck-review).
- **Except for questions, route back to the ledger and the rules** (「修正の指示」 in AGENTS.md; how to choose the destination: [rules.md](rules.md)). Put `ledger.py add` and the rule edits into one call (one `bash`). Write in the outline only when the policy changed (content/structure, major, or premise fixes), and do not copy the correction itself there (it is in the ledger; "Routing corrections" in rules.md).
- **If the speaker notes changed,** update the video made from the deck, if any, without asking; if the pages changed, say instead that the video no longer matches ("When to make or update a video" in `../vids-beta/SKILL.md`).
- **Deliver.** The URL (rebuilding does not change it), the pages fixed and what was fixed, what was checked (what was run, and why if no child agent was started), the questions for the user, and which correction was routed to which rule. If you only answered a question, the answer and the source are enough. Before delivering, call the context-summarizing tool if you can (deck-practices-005).

Evidence: 67 revision requests after delivery (2026-10-09 to 10-19, pi logs; the workspace's `Internal/deck_revision_speed_20261019/`) took a median of 13.2 minutes and $2.41 each. The 26 that started a review child agent took 23.9 minutes and $3.76; the 41 that did not took 8.2 minutes and $1.90. Of 19 fixes that pointed at a page, 4 started a child agent, and in 2 of them the child agent raised 「直す」 (4 in total). Of the 35 child agents, including those for fixes that did not point at a page, 26 raised at least one 「直す」, so dropping child agents entirely is risky. The median number of builds per request was 3, and writing to the ledger, the rules, and the outline took about 3 minutes per request.

## What not to do

- Do not make slides via HTML, Marp, or Slidev. The quality of the editable slides drops. Using them for drafts or for exploring the look is fine (AI 実践 2 節).
- Do not include slides made with NotebookLM or image generation in deliverables. Every page becomes an image and cannot be edited in Google Slides. When you use them as material, check that their text appears in the `deck.md` of `gslides.py check` (AI 実践 1-8).
- Do not have a model that looks at images score the visual quality. Its correlation with human ratings is low (研究 1-3). Have it list defects.
- Do not use AI design suggestions (such as "Design Ideas" in the official pptx skill) that do not suit Japanese text (visual.md §8).
