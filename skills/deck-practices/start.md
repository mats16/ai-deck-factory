# Steps 1–5: preparation and agreeing on the structure

Steps 1–5 of "Overall flow" in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md), for decks and documents. After 5, decks continue with steps 6–8 in `../slides-build/workflow.md`; documents continue with `../docs-build/SKILL.md`.

## 1. Decide the folder

If none is given, decide it from the request and tell the user in the first reply (ask only when you cannot tell whether the material is for a customer or internal). Customer-facing: `Customers/<customer>/<content>_<date>/`; internal: `Internal/<content>_<date>/`; research that produces no material: `Research/` (「調べもの」 in AGENTS.md). The date is the meeting or submission date; if unknown, this month. Do not create files directly under the workspace root (「フォルダの置き方」 in AGENTS.md). If the folder already exists, read its contents first (`構成案*.md`, `社内メモ_*.md`, `references.md`, `build/`).

- [deck-flow-002] **Folder names:** one folder per material. Name it `<content>_<date>` in lowercase English (e.g. `Customers/<customer>/sample_briefing_202610/`). Create a folder even for a single-file material. Revisions of the same material go in the same folder. Things remade for every regular meeting (schedules, etc.) get a new folder each time (e.g. `schedule_20261007/`, `schedule_20260930/`). Internal materials (study sessions, meetups, enablement) that do not belong to a customer go in `Internal/`.
- **Folder contents:** do not keep decks and documents locally; build them as Google Slides and Docs on Drive (the URL goes in the outline). Whether to delete `build/` is decided in "What to keep" in [deliver.md](deliver.md).
  ```
  構成案.md               … if any
  社内メモ_<content>.md   … things not shown to the customer (speaking order, 要確認 items, personal names, internal circumstances)
  build/                  … source of truth for the content (decks: deck.json and one file per page p<number>.json [older materials: pages.py]; documents: doc.md). Images used only by this material go in build/assets/
  references.md           … ledger of the sources read while making it (one row per source with an S<number> ID; `refs.py add`; format: "Ledger" in materials.md)
  ```

## 2. Read past materials for the same customer

Other materials in the customer folder and `社内メモ_*.md` describe the counterpart and the history; the customer's `memory.md` holds preferences that apply across materials (the hook shows it after you open `構成案*.md` etc.; "Customer memory" in `rules.md`). Also open the customer's `profile.md` (business, organization, technical setup, engineers' preferences). If it is missing or stale ("Going stale" in `customer-profile`), create or refresh it with the `customer-profile` skill before the outline.

## 3. Find internal materials

Search `references_index.md` first. Then find internal decks, documents, and chat discussions on the same theme, and decide what to reuse and which messages to align with ([materials.md](materials.md); how to reuse: [reuse.md](../slides-build/reuse.md)).

## 4. Gather customer information

From meeting records and internal chat (`[sources]` in `factory.toml`), pick up the customer's interests, the history, and what was discussed so far ([materials.md](materials.md)). Meetings cited before are in `references_index.md`. Add every source you read in 3 and 4 to `references.md` with `refs.py add`, and note each fact you may use with the source ID and the place in it (「S3 の Regions の表」) ("Ledger" in materials.md). Child agents that read sources add their rows the same way and return facts with the IDs.

## 5. Agree on the structure

[deck-flow-003] **Agree on the structure.** For every material, before starting, confirm with the user the material type, the audience, the audience's question, and what the audience can do after reading (「いい資料の考え方」 in AGENTS.md). Confirm even if you could write them from the request. Open [content.md](content.md) before you confirm them, because its §1 and deck-content-039 decide what to ask for each material type (such as the builder's intent for materials that introduce a mechanism). The review after the plan (the outline) is decided follows `[review] plan_reviewer` in `factory.toml`. `"ai"` means ① AI reviews and you go on to build; `"human"` means ② a human reviews (if unset, ①). If the user names the other one for this material, use that. Write which one you chose in the outline's premises. The default is ①, and the human checks only the first four questions (the three representative pages, the check on every build, and `/deck-review` are also AI). ② is kept because the transition is still in progress. Materials that explain this mechanism should say so too. For decks over 10 pages, long documents, or when the counterpart or the purpose is unclear, write `構成案.md` before making the material (otherwise pass the four answers and the plan to the review). Write the key points per page for decks and per chapter for documents, and list reused internal materials in 「流用元」 at the end with URL and page number. When revising, bump the number, as in `構成案_v2.md`.

- **Choose a theme.** Themes are the workspace's theme files (`themes/slides/<name>.json`; built-in: visual and company) and document theme files (`themes/docs/<name>.json`); choose by their `description` (modeled on how Agent Skills selects a skill). `slides.py themes` (for documents, `docs.py themes`) prints the name, whether it is the default, whether it has a template, and the `description`. Match the `description` against the material type, the audience, and any company color requirement, and write 「テーマ: <名前>（<選んだ理由>）」 in the outline's premises. The default theme is for when the `description` does not decide it. When the choice is not decided, conflicts with the user's instruction, or you want a theme that has no template, ask together with the structure questions. For decks, write it in `theme` in `build/deck.json` (`new` creates the deck with that theme, and `lint` and `dry` check with it; `../slides-build/themes.md`). For documents, write `theme:` at the top of doc.md (`theme: standard` if left as default). If there are two or more themes and you run `new` without choosing, it prints the list and stops.
- **Write three things for each page in the outline.** The heading (a short noun phrase and one sentence it can claim), the page type (parallel, contrast, progression, structure, etc.; `../slides-build/visual.md` §6), and the evidence with its source. Choose the type page by page from what the page says, not by copying the representative pages or a past deck (slides-visual-048, 049). [deck-flow-012] For pages with an assertive heading (the 2–3 pages that ask for a decision or conclude with a number), write 「言い切り」 in the type column (content.md §4). Write every fact the pages will state (numbers, computed values, availability, the customer's environment, proper nouns, and our own `見立て`) as rows of the 「出典の表」 at the end of the outline (columns and kinds: "Ledger" in materials.md; deck-content-064). Run `refs.py check <material folder>` and fix its format errors. lint reports numbers on pages that are not in the table, and the review child agent in 9 compares the table with the numbers on the slides (aspect 2 in `../deck-review/prompt_slides.md`. Do not let a model that looks at images judge numeric errors; compare them with the source text. 研究 1-10).
- **Read only the headings and the claims in order, and check that the story holds.** When the outline is written, the parent reads them and also has the plan-review child agent look. After building, the review child agent in 9 reads the list of headings and leads that the lint in `gslides.py check` prints (aspect 1. 中身 1-1、研究 1-1・1-2).
- [deck-flow-019] **Check the facts against the sources in the background.** Right after writing the 「出典の表」, start a source-check child agent with pass 1 of `../deck-review/prompt_sources.md`, in the same message as the plan-review child. Start pass 2 in 9, together with round 1 of `/deck-review`. Do not wait for either (deck-agents-013): keep writing and building, and take the results in at the next point where you put in fixes (the three-page fixes in 6, a review round's fixes in 9). For each row: `合う` → set `確認` to `照合済`; `違う` or `言い過ぎ` → fix the row and the same value on every page, in `authorNotes`, and in any notes (search `build/*.json`), and list it on delivery, but if the fix breaks a heading's claim or the page's point, leave the page and ask in 10 (deck-flow-010); `確かめられない` → ask in 10, and in customer-facing materials do not state it as fact. If the child lists items under 「見ていない」, start another child on those, also in the background. If a pass has not returned by delivery, deliver without waiting, list its rows as 「照合中」 in the report, and put the results in as a content/structure fix when it returns. The findings file is `/tmp/sources_<material folder name>_<1 or 2>.md`; write the path and the child's task ID in the outline, so that they survive summarizing. Evidence: "Ledger" in materials.md (the maker's 「済」 and the review child agents missed errors whose source was weak or that were not in the table at all).
- **Plan review:** for every material, run it the way `plan_reviewer` says.
  - **② Human:** show the outline (for materials without an outline, the four answers and the plan) and get confirmation.
  - [deck-flow-004] **① AI:** give a new subagent without the conversation the user's original request (you may format and organize it, but do not change its meaning), the material's direction (`構成案*.md`, or the four answers and the plan if there is none), and the paths to `社内メモ_*.md` and `references.md` (for it to open when it wants to check sources). Do not pass the conversation or the raw sources (minutes, transcripts, etc.). They pull it toward the maker's thinking and weaken the point of a second pair of eyes. Before passing, write everything decided in the conversation and every rejected idea into the outline or the internal memo. How to ask: [agents.md](agents.md). The request text: "Read the original request and the direction, check whether the direction answers the request, and list only the gaps and omissions. Check: whether the audience, the question, and what the audience can do after reading match the request; what is in the request but missing from the direction, and what was added though not in the request; whether the story holds when reading only the headings in order; and whether it departs from the six points of 「いい資料の考え方」 in AGENTS.md (「主役は聞き手」「問いを先に」「煽らない」「新しい見方」「公平」「見解」)." While waiting, start writing the page JSON of the three representative pages (for decks that skip them, the whole) (deck-agents-013). Verify the findings yourself; for those you may fix, fix the outline and go straight on to 6. For those that change the audience, the question, or the claim, change nothing, proceed, and put them in the list of questions for the user in 10. Record the findings received and the fixes made at the end of the outline.
- After the plan review, call the context-summarizing tool if you can (deck-flow-005).
